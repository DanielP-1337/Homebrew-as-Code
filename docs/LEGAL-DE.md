# Brewing law and beer tax: Germany

> **Jurisdiction: Germany. Reviewed: 2026-10-19.** Informational only;
> not legal or tax advice. Rules can change. Confirm current requirements
> with the competent Hauptzollamt and other relevant authorities before brewing,
> distributing, or selling beer.

## Private homebrewing

Under **§ 41(1) Biersteuerverordnung (BierStV)**, beer produced by home and
hobby brewers in their households **exclusively for their own consumption and
not sold** is exempt from German beer tax for up to **5 hectolitres (500 L)
per calendar year**. The regulation also addresses non-commercial community
brewhouses. This is not a blanket exemption for every producer, every
recipient, or every use of beer. In particular, do not interpret the rule
as an unconditional allowance *per person* without checking the specific
circumstances.

**Effective 2025-01-01**, the former advance notification requirement for
exempt home/hobby brewing was removed. The former **200 L** threshold and
mandatory advance brewing notice are outdated for the above exemption.

Under **§ 41(2) BierStV**, exceeding the exempt quantity requires a beer-tax
return using the prescribed form. Other uses (in particular sales or
commercial production) are not covered by the § 41(1) exemption and require
separate assessment. The fact that an event is free of charge does not by
itself establish tax-exempt personal consumption; ask the customs office
about tastings, contests and events if uncertain.

## Commercial and public use

If a recipe is transferred to a commercial brewery or used for a company
event, wedding, public event, or Christmas market, the producer/organizer
must determine applicable beer-tax, manufacturing, food-law, labeling,
licensing and sales requirements. The applicable rules depend on the
specific arrangement; do not use the homebrew exemption as a substitute.

## Project boundaries

- `recipe.yaml` describes **what to brew**, not tax status or permission to brew.
- Exporters, shopping lists, supplier RFQs and equipment adapters **do not**
  confer authorization, complete customs filings or determine tax liability.
- Actual brew logs, annual quantities and legal records should be tracked
  separately from the portable recipe domain and checked for the relevant
  jurisdiction. Do not commit personal tax identifiers or correspondence.
- No compliance checks for other countries are currently implemented.
  For brewing outside Germany, consult official local guidance.
